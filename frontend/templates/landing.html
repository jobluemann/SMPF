What SMPF does

🔗

Connect everything

One-click OAuth for X, Meta, YouTube, Reddit, Threads, Minds, VK, and Google Drive. No API keys to copy. Just connect and go.

🎨

AI content generation

Generate images with Pollinations.ai, voice with Groq Orpheus, and text with OpenRouter — all free-tier, no credit card required.

📊

Live analytics

Track which platforms are connected, how much content you've generated, and monitor everything from a clean dashboard with Chart.js.

Supported platforms

X (Twitter)

OAuth 2.0 with PKCE. Post text, images, threads.

Facebook

Page posting via Graph API. Link Instagram Business accounts.

Instagram

Direct Instagram Login for Business/Creator accounts.

Threads

Meta's Threads API. Post text and image threads.

YouTube

Google OAuth. Upload videos and manage your channel.

Reddit

Subreddit posting, comment management, karma tracking.

Minds

Decentralized social. OAuth2 posting and analytics.

Google Drive

Auto-backup generated content to your Drive folder.
